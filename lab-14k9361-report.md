# AGNTCY: A Comprehensive Framework for AI Agent Collaboration

## Purpose
AGNTCY was developed to address the challenges of isolated AI agents that cannot collaborate across organizational boundaries. Its primary goal is to create an "Internet of Agents" (IoA), where AI agents can discover, verify, and collaborate securely. The vision is to accelerate human work by enabling enterprises to create workflows and applications that combine internal and third-party agents, thereby achieving significant productivity gains.

## Features
### 1. Identity Management
- Provides a centralized hub for managing and verifying digital identities of agents.
- Supports both verification of existing identities and registration of new ones.
- Ensures secure interactions through decentralized identity technologies and verifiable credentials.

### 2. Core Components
- **Open Agent Schema Framework (OASF)**: A data model for describing agents' attributes and ensuring unique identification.
- **Agent Directory**: Facilitates the discovery of agents and multi-agent applications.
- **Messaging SDK (SLIM)**: A protocol for secure and efficient communication between agents.
- **Observability and Evaluation**: Tools for monitoring and improving multi-agent systems.
- **Security**: Robust mechanisms to protect multi-agent applications.

### 3. Interoperability
- Establishes common protocols for communication between agents from different vendors and platforms.
- Supports standards like W3C Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) and Verifiable Credentials (VCs).

### 4. Scalability
- Designed to support a growing number of agents and interactions without compromising performance.

### 5. Standardization
- Develops standardized data models and schemas for consistent data representation across the ecosystem.

## Use Cases
### 1. Enterprise Workflows
- Enables the creation of agentic workflows that combine internal and external agents for business acceleration.

### 2. Multi-Agent Applications
- Facilitates the development of applications where multiple agents collaborate to achieve complex tasks.

### 3. Secure Communication
- Ensures secure and trustworthy interactions between agents, preventing unauthorized access and data breaches.

### 4. Identity Verification
- Verifies the authenticity of agents and their capabilities, ensuring reliable operations in multi-agent systems.

## Origins and Vision
AGNTCY originated from Outshift by Cisco's vision of an Internet of Agents. It was launched in March 2025 with contributions from organizations like Galileo and LangChain. By July 2025, it was donated to the Linux Foundation, becoming a community-owned infrastructure supported by major companies like Cisco, Dell Technologies, Google Cloud, Oracle, and Red Hat.

The vision is to create an open, interoperable IoA that accelerates innovation and creates value for all participants, from developers to consumers across industries.
