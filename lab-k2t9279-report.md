# AGNTCY: Building the Internet of Agents

## Origins
AGNTCY was initiated by Outshift, a division of Cisco, with the vision of creating an "Internet of Agents." The goal was to address the challenges of isolated AI agents that could not collaborate across organizational boundaries. The foundational white paper identified critical infrastructure gaps preventing agents from working together at scale.

In March 2025, Outshift launched AGNTCY on GitHub, providing complete code, specifications, and services. Core maintainers included Galileo and LangChain, who developed essential components for discovery, identity, messaging, and observability. By July 2025, AGNTCY was donated to the Linux Foundation, with Cisco, Dell Technologies, Google Cloud, Oracle, and Red Hat as formative members. This marked the transition from a company-driven initiative to a community-owned infrastructure.

## Vision and Mission
### Vision
AGNTCY aims to accelerate human work by enabling enterprises to create agentic workflows and applications that combine internal and third-party agents. The ultimate goal is to leverage AI to drive innovation and productivity across industries.

### Mission
AGNTCY is an open-source collective dedicated to building the Internet of Agents. Its mission is to create a collaborative space for innovation, development, and maintenance of software components and services that address key challenges in agentic workflows and multi-agent applications.

## Key Components
### SLIM (Secure Low-Latency Interactive Messaging)
SLIM is a protocol that defines standards for secure and efficient communication between AI agents. It ensures interoperability and seamless data exchange through:
- Message formats
- Transport mechanisms
- Interaction patterns

SLIM Nodes and SDKs provide secure network-level communication services to groups of agents, extending gRPC to support various messaging patterns like pub/sub, request/reply, streaming, and fire-and-forget.

### Identity
AGNTCY leverages decentralized technologies to manage and verify the identities of agents or tools issued by organizations. This ensures secure and trustworthy interactions.

### Observability and Evaluation
Telemetry collectors and tools enable observability and evaluation of multi-agent applications, ensuring their reliability and performance.

### Security
AGNTCY provides tools and services to protect and trust multi-agent applications, safeguarding them against malicious activities.

### CoffeeAGNTCY
CoffeeAGNTCY is a reference implementation that demonstrates the core components of AGNTCY, showcasing how multi-agent applications can be built effectively.

## Benefits
- Enhanced collaboration between agents
- Secure and trustworthy interactions
- Improved observability and evaluation
- Accelerated innovation and productivity

## Conclusion
AGNTCY represents a significant step forward in the development of multi-agent systems. By providing a robust infrastructure for discovery, identity, messaging, and observability, it enables agents to collaborate securely and efficiently, driving innovation across industries.
