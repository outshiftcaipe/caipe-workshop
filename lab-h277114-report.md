# AGNTCY Report

AGNTCY is a groundbreaking initiative aimed at creating an open, interoperable Internet of Agents (IoA). Below is a detailed overview of its concept, applications, and significance:

## Concept
AGNTCY originated from Outshift by Cisco's vision of an "Internet of Agents," addressing the challenge of isolated AI agents unable to collaborate across organizational boundaries. The initiative was launched in March 2025 on GitHub, with contributions from Galileo and LangChain as core maintainers. It focuses on building essential components like discovery, identity, messaging, and observability to enable agents to find, verify, and collaborate securely.

By July 2025, AGNTCY was donated to the Linux Foundation, with major companies like Cisco, Dell Technologies, Google Cloud, Oracle, and Red Hat as formative members. This transition marked its evolution from a single company's vision to a community-owned infrastructure.

## Applications
AGNTCY provides a robust framework for creating multi-agent applications and workflows. Its capabilities include:
1. **Discover**: Identifying and evaluating the best agents for specific tasks.
2. **Compose**: Connecting agents into effective workflows across various frameworks and vendors.
3. **Deploy**: Running multi-agent systems securely and at scale.
4. **Evaluate**: Monitoring performance and improving efficiency over time.

## Core Components
AGNTCY's architecture includes several key components:
- **Open Agent Schema Framework (OASF)**: A data model for describing agents' attributes and ensuring unique identification.
- **Agent Directory**: A system for announcing and discovering agents or multi-agent applications.
- **Messaging SDK (SLIM)**: A protocol for secure and efficient communication between AI agents.
- **Identity System**: Decentralized technologies for managing and verifying agent identities.
- **Observability and Evaluation Tools**: Telemetry collectors and services for monitoring multi-agent applications.
- **Security Tools**: Mechanisms to protect and trust multi-agent applications.

## Significance
AGNTCY is significant for several reasons:
1. **Enhanced Collaboration**: It fosters seamless communication and data exchange between AI agents, enabling sophisticated and integrated solutions.
2. **Improved Efficiency**: Standardized protocols simplify the integration of diverse AI agents, accelerating development and deployment.
3. **Increased Security**: Robust security mechanisms protect sensitive data and prevent unauthorized access.
4. **Future-Proof Architecture**: Its scalable and flexible design ensures adaptability to future advancements in AI technology.

## Vision and Mission
AGNTCY envisions accelerating human work through agentic AI, enabling enterprises to create workflows and applications that combine internal and third-party agents for significant productivity gains. Its mission is to build an accessible Internet of Agents through open-source collaboration, solving key problems in agentic workflows and multi-agent applications.
