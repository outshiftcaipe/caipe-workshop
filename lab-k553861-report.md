# AGNTCY Report

## Origins
AGNTCY was initiated by Outshift, a division of Cisco, with the vision of creating an "Internet of Agents". This concept aimed to address the challenges of isolated AI agents unable to collaborate across organizational boundaries. The foundational white paper highlighted the infrastructure gap preventing agents from working together at scale.

In March 2025, Outshift launched AGNTCY on GitHub, providing complete code, specifications, and services. Core maintainers included Galileo and LangChain, who developed essential components for discovery, identity, messaging, and observability. By July 2025, AGNTCY was donated to the Linux Foundation, with Cisco, Dell Technologies, Google Cloud, Oracle, and Red Hat as formative members. This transition marked AGNTCY's evolution into a community-owned infrastructure.

## Vision and Mission
### Vision
AGNTCY envisions an open, interoperable Internet of Agents to accelerate innovation and create value for all participants, including developers, operators, and consumers across industries.

### Mission
The mission of AGNTCY is to build a diverse, collaborative space for innovating, developing, and maintaining software components and services that solve key problems in agentic workflows and multi-agent applications.

## Key Components
### SLIM (Secure Low-latency Interactive Messaging)
SLIM is a protocol defining standards for secure and efficient communication between AI agents. It ensures interoperability and seamless data exchange through specified message formats, transport mechanisms, and interaction patterns.

### Identity
AGNTCY leverages decentralized technologies to manage and verify the identities of agents or tools issued by organizations. This ensures secure and trustworthy interactions.

### Observability and Evaluation
Telemetry collectors, tools, and services enable observability and evaluation of multi-agent applications.

### Security
AGNTCY provides tools and services to trust and protect multi-agent applications.

### CoffeeAGNTCY
CoffeeAGNTCY is a reference implementation demonstrating the core components for building multi-agent applications.

## Benefits
- Enhanced collaboration between agents.
- Secure and trustworthy interactions.
- Improved observability and evaluation of applications.
- Accelerated innovation and productivity gains.

## Conclusion
AGNTCY represents a significant step forward in creating a collaborative ecosystem for AI agents. By addressing key challenges in identity, messaging, and observability, it paves the way for a more interconnected and efficient digital landscape.
