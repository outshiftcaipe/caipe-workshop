# AGNTCY: Building the Internet of Agents

## Origins
AGNTCY was initiated by Outshift, a division of Cisco, with the vision of creating an "Internet of Agents." The goal was to address the challenges of isolated AI agents that could not collaborate across organizational boundaries. The foundational white paper identified critical infrastructure gaps that hindered agents from working together at scale.

In March 2025, AGNTCY was launched on GitHub with complete code, specifications, and services. Core maintainers included Galileo and LangChain. The project focused on building essential components such as discovery, identity, messaging, and observability to enable agents to find each other, verify capabilities, and collaborate securely.

By July 2025, over 75 companies joined the initiative, leading to AGNTCY's donation to the Linux Foundation. Founding members included Cisco, Dell Technologies, Google Cloud, Oracle, and Red Hat. This marked the transition of AGNTCY from a company-driven project to a community-owned infrastructure.

## Vision and Mission
### Vision
AGNTCY envisions a future where agentic AI accelerates human work across industries. The aim is to create agentic workflows and applications that combine internal and third-party agents to maximize productivity and innovation.

### Mission
The mission of AGNTCY is to build an open, interoperable Internet of Agents accessible to all. It fosters a collaborative space for innovation, development, and maintenance of software components and services that address key challenges in agentic workflows and multi-agent applications.

## Core Capabilities
1. **SLIM (Secure Low-Latency Interactive Messaging):**
   - A protocol for secure and efficient communication between AI agents.
   - Ensures interoperability through standardized message formats, transport mechanisms, and interaction patterns.
   - Extends gRPC to support pub/sub interactions, request/reply, streaming, and more.

2. **Identity Management:**
   - Uses decentralized technologies to manage and verify agent identities.
   - Ensures secure and trustworthy interactions between agents.

3. **Observability and Evaluation:**
   - Provides telemetry collectors and tools for monitoring and evaluating multi-agent applications.

4. **Security:**
   - Offers tools and services to protect and establish trust in multi-agent applications.

5. **CoffeeAGNTCY:**
   - A reference implementation demonstrating the core components for building multi-agent applications.

## Benefits
- **Enhanced Collaboration:** Facilitates seamless interaction between agents across organizational boundaries.
- **Scalability:** Supports large-scale, distributed multi-agent systems.
- **Security:** Ensures secure communication and identity verification.
- **Innovation:** Provides a platform for developing advanced agentic workflows and applications.

## Conclusion
AGNTCY represents a significant step toward realizing the vision of an Internet of Agents. By addressing the challenges of interoperability, security, and scalability, it lays the foundation for a future where AI agents can collaborate effectively to drive innovation and productivity.
