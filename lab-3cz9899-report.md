## AGNTCY Research Report

### Origins
AGNTCY originated from Cisco's Outshift initiative, envisioning an **Internet of Agents** to address the isolation of AI agents in silos. The foundational white paper highlighted the need for infrastructure enabling collaboration at scale. AGNTCY was launched in March 2025 on GitHub, featuring discovery, identity, messaging, and observability components. By July 2025, it was donated to the Linux Foundation, becoming a community-owned infrastructure supported by major companies like Cisco, Dell Technologies, Google Cloud, Oracle, and Red Hat.

### Vision
AGNTCY aims to accelerate human work through **Agentic AI**, enabling enterprises to create workflows combining internal and third-party agents. The vision emphasizes an open, interoperable Internet of Agents to drive innovation and productivity across industries.

### Mission
AGNTCY is an open-source collective dedicated to building accessible infrastructure for the Internet of Agents. Its mission is to foster collaboration and innovation in agentic workflows and multi-agent applications.

---

### Capabilities
AGNTCY offers tools and services for multi-agent applications:
- **Discovery**: Locating and verifying agents.
- **Identity**: Secure, decentralized identity management.
- **Messaging**: Low-latency communication protocols.
- **Observability**: Telemetry tools for monitoring applications.
- **Security**: Trust and protection services.

---

### SLIM (Secure Low-Latency Interactive Messaging)
SLIM is a protocol for secure, efficient communication between AI agents, ensuring interoperability and seamless data exchange.

#### Components
1. **SLIM Messaging Layer**: Provides scalable messaging infrastructure.
2. **SLIM Nodes and SDK**: Enables secure communication with features like pub/sub, request/reply, and streaming.

#### Integration with OpenTelemetry
SLIM integrates with OpenTelemetry for observability, offering:
- Custom telemetry components.
- Tools for generating traces, metrics, and logs.
- Docker-based deployment for SLIM nodes.

#### Benefits
SLIM enhances collaboration, scalability, and security, supporting AGNTCY's vision for the Internet of Agents.
